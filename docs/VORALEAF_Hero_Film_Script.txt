VORALEAF HERO FILM SCRIPT
THE COFFEE MOMENT
24-second live-action production brief
24 October 2026

The current website uses an original animated-still motion study. This brief is for a separate real live-action shoot. Final preparation shots must follow signed supplier instructions. No filmed product pour is supplied by this document.

0-3 s
PICTURE: Macro view of a VORALEAF paper envelope on a textured surface. Light travels across the print as an adult hand enters.
SOUND AND WORDS: Soft paper movement. No spoken line.

3-6 s
PICTURE: The hand opens the envelope and removes the inner pouch. A men’s edition shows gold; a separate BLOOM cut shows rose gold.
SOUND AND WORDS: A short pouch sound. Voiceover: “A moment, made yours.”

6-9 s
PICTURE: Approved product is dispensed into a clean ceramic cup. Keep a practical angle and omit this shot until directions are confirmed.
SOUND AND WORDS: Natural pour or granule sound. No ingredient or benefit caption.

9-14 s
PICTURE: Real water or coffee pours in one continuous, physically credible stream. Capture ripples and a gentle plume of steam.
SOUND AND WORDS: Close-recorded liquid sound. On-screen: “ROOTS HERBAL COFFEE”.

14-18 s
PICTURE: A slow stir and a still cup in warm light. Cut to a person settling beside a window or after an ordinary walk.
SOUND AND WORDS: Room ambience. Voiceover: “Make room for your coffee moment.”

18-21 s
PICTURE: Three approved edition packs appear on the same surface. Keep all labels readable and the products at a believable scale.
SOUND AND WORDS: Subtle musical resolve. On-screen: “BOLD   EMBER   BLOOM”.

21-24 s
PICTURE: Hold the VORALEAF identity beside the cup, with open space for the site’s live HTML button.
SOUND AND WORDS: On-screen: “Explore the collection”. End on a frame that returns gently to the opening light.

CAPTURE AND DELIVERY
Shoot a 4K master at 25 fps for standard motion, with dedicated 50 or 100 fps pour takes where lighting supports clean slow motion. Use a controlled tabletop, tripod or slider, macro lens, soft side light and a real food stylist. Record clean pouring, paper and cup sounds separately. Obtain performer and location permissions and commercial music rights.
Deliver a 16:9 desktop film, a separate 9:16 mobile composition and a poster image. Encode an H.264 MP4 and a tested WebM alternative. Make an 8-12 second silent loop for the background, preserving the full 24-second version behind an explicit play control. Keep text and buttons in HTML, provide pause and captions for spoken versions, and use a still poster for reduced motion or data saving.
