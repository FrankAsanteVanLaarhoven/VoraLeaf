## Avoid / reject

- Fused outer/inner packaging, a paper inner pouch, exposed powder inside the outer envelope, or resealable zips absent from the reference.
- Yellow-gold BLOOM inner pouches, missing sachets, swapped edition names, changed logos or invented brand/certification seals.
- Extra fingers, floating hands, broken grips, intersecting kettle/cup geometry, leaking sealed pouches or water poured into a closed sachet.
- Coffee liquid pouring out of an instant-powder pouch, disappearing powder, impossible water flow or abrupt package size changes.
- “Halal certified”, “organic”, recycling claims or manufacturer benefits unless separately evidenced and approved for this exact product.
- Fake before/after health results, breathlessness treatment, weight-loss results, desire/stamina outcomes or medical authority cues.
- Third-party music, social platform logos or unsupported endorsements.
